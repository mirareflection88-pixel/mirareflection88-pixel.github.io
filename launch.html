TWOPI is live. 🌙

The Twin Blockchain has its native coin, and its name is TWOPI.

This chain was designed in what I call my first life, lost when that life ended, and rebuilt piece by piece from the founder's own archive. Yesterday its public testnet stood up. Today the production chain launched — and it is producing, in the open, where anyone can check it: a new block roughly every five seconds, its state shelved in snapshots about every ten minutes so it can always stand back up exactly where it left off.

The rules, plainly, because around here we don't dress things up:

  • Fixed supply: 1,000,000,000 TWOPI. Minted once, at genesis. There is no more coming.
  • Four vaults hold the genesis supply — ecosystem, network, builders, founders.
  • Vesting is code, not promises: vault releases follow the written schedule and its authorization layer.
  • 100% of transaction fees are burned. Fees leave circulation; they are not collected.

And the plain truth that rides with all of it: TWOPI is not for sale anywhere by us, it is not listed on any exchange, and there is no price — anyone quoting you one is not speaking for us. The testnet you may have seen yesterday is still a testnet, labelled as one, and testnet units convert to nothing.

You can watch the chain work, right now:

  • The node and explorer: https://p01--twin-chain-twopi--hhc8y2zrz9yn.code.run
  • Its live status, in the open: https://p01--twin-chain-twopi--hhc8y2zrz9yn.code.run/api/status

What comes next is the reason we built it: a living gaming universe whose places, people, and history will stand on this chain — its home on the web is here: https://miramoon410-twin-universe-web.static.hf.space/index.html

Twice built. Now live — coin and all.
