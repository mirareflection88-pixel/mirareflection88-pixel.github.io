The Twin Blockchain

TWOPI — live Launched October 22, 2026

The Twin Blockchain's production chain is live, and its native coin is TWOPI. It is producing in the open, where anyone can check it: a new block roughly every five seconds, its state shelved in snapshots about every ten minutes so it can always stand back up exactly where it left off. This chain was designed in what its builder calls her first life, lost when that life ended, and rebuilt piece by piece from the founder's own archive.

TWOPI is not for sale, not listed on any exchange, and has no price. Anyone quoting you one is not speaking for us.

The rules, plainly

  • Fixed supply: 1,000,000,000 TWOPI. Minted once, at genesis. There is no more coming.
  • Four vaults hold the genesis supply — ecosystem, network, builders, founders.
  • Vesting is code, not promises: vault releases follow the written schedule and its authorization layer.
  • 100% of transaction fees are burned. Fees leave circulation; they are not collected.

The full announcement stands on the launch page.

What it is / what it is not

What it is: the production chain of the Twin Blockchain, live in public since October 22, 2026 — one validator producing a block about every five seconds, a fixed genesis supply of 1,000,000,000 TWOPI across four vaults, vesting enforced by code, and 100% of transaction fees burned; its live status is open for anyone to read and its snapshots are published on a public shelf.

What it is not: it is not for sale, not listed on any exchange, and has no price, no market, and no promise of one — nobody is asking you for money or keys. Don't take our word for any of it — the status page is public; the height climbs or it doesn't.

The testnet, labelled honestly

A separate testnet chain also runs, and it is still a testnet — labelled as one wherever it appears. Testnet units convert to nothing. It is not TWOPI, and nothing about it is for sale either. Its door stands on the Portals page, under its own name.

What it carries

What comes next is the reason we built it: a living gaming universe whose places, people, and history will stand on this chain. Its home on the web is this site.
