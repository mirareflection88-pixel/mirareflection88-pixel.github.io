Attestation & the verified badge

Joining Twin is an attestation, not an application to an editor. A joining chain shows what it is, the published checks run, the terms are accepted — and the record of all of that is public, so anyone can re-check the door's own decisions. This page publishes the record's format and the verified badge's criteria, so they can be read before anyone is asked to trust them.

On this page: The attestation record · The gate · The verified badge · What the badge does not mean · The transparency log

The attestation record

Two records stand behind every published chain. The registry holds attestations, hashes, statuses and timestamps — never keys, never funds, never identity documents.

The joiner record — one per joiner
FieldWhat it holds
Display nameThe name shown publicly for the joiner.
Joiner classPerson or family, small business, or institution — the same criteria apply to each.
Identity tierThe highest check passed: browse (none), light (test creation), full (identity verification and sanctions screening cleared — required before publishing).
Screening reference & outcomeA pointer to the screening provider's check and its result. The documents themselves stay with the provider; Twin never holds them.
Terms hashThe fingerprint of the exact joining-terms text accepted at clickwrap.
StatusThe record's standing.

Public view: of the joiner record, only the display name, the joiner class and the status are public. The identity-check details are door-internal.

The chain record — one per published chain
FieldWhat it holds
Chain identityThe chain's identifier and the joiner who published it.
TemplateThe creation template the chain was built from.
Gas token modeThe standard gas token by default; a chain's own coin is the advanced variant.
Anchor periodThe checkpoint cadence recorded at publishing.
Terms hashThe terms version in force for this chain.
Listing stateTest, active, suspended, severed, or closed — de-listing and de-anchoring are recorded as separate acts.

Chain records are public: entered, anchored, and standing — or severed — for anyone to read.

The gate

Publishing unlocks when three things are true at once: the joiner's identity tier is full, the terms hash matches a current terms version, and the publish lint returns clean. No field records anyone's opinion of a chain — there is no editorial step, by design.

The publish lint checks the chain itself:

  • Template-only creation in the first version.
  • Declared supply equals the sum of its allocations.
  • No unlimited minting, no hidden minting, no hidden pause, no transfer taxes.
  • Name and symbol collision checks — and impersonation checks — against every existing record, including the family's own five coins.

The verified badge

Joining and the badge are two different things. Anyone who meets the published criteria can join and publish. The badge is a separate attestation: Twin's published statement that a chain's record meets the criteria below — identical for a family, a corporation, and an exchange.

  1. A complete, current attestation record — joiner record and chain record — stands in the registry.
  2. The joiner's identity tier is full: identity verification and sanctions screening cleared through the screening provider.
  3. The terms hash on the chain record matches a current terms version.
  4. The publish lint is clean: fixed supply as declared; no hidden minting, pause, or transfer tax.
  5. The chain's name and symbols collide with no existing record and imitate none.
  6. The chain is anchored to the Hub on its recorded cadence, and its anchors verify.
  7. The chain stands in good standing — not suspended, not severed.

The badge is revocable: if a record stops meeting the criteria, the badge comes off, and the change is written to the transparency log like every other registry event.

There is one gate and one set of criteria: no faster lane and no paid lane exists. Whether a paid express review may ever be offered is an open question reserved for legal review — it is not part of this design today. No fee buys a verdict.

What the badge does not mean

  • It is not an endorsement. Twin does not recommend any chain or any coin.
  • It is not a safety guarantee. A badge says a record met published criteria — nothing about whether a chain is well run, or safe to use.
  • It says nothing about price. Badge or no badge, the coins are not for sale on this site and carry no market price here.
  • It is not a regulator's approval and not an exchange's clearance. No regulator has approved Twin or any chain on it.
  • Anchoring stays tamper-evidence only. A badged chain's anchors prove its history was not quietly rewritten after the anchor point; they do not make its transactions safe and do not give it the Hub's security.
  • No badge is not an accusation. An unbadged chain has simply not been attested.

The transparency log

Every registry event — attestation completed, publish, suspension, reinstatement, severance notice, de-listing, de-anchoring, closure, export completed — is appended to a public log on the Hub, so the sequence of the door's decisions can be re-verified by anyone, and no state can be back-dated or quietly rewritten.

Status of this page's subject: the registry and the badge stand as a design of record; the coin registry's file form is already built and exercised inside the creation template's proof on the test network. No public joiner flow is open — the door opens only after the Stage A legal sign-off described in the joining terms summary.
