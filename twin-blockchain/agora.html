AGORA — AGORA

AGORA is the family's commons — named for the public square. It is the largest of the four coins and the only one that pays out slowly as it runs: its rewards account releases a small amount with every block, so its balance falls as the chain grows.

How much exists — fixed forever

500,000,000 AGORA is the fixed cap — no more can ever be made. Coins move out of the rewards account as emission, but the total across all accounts never passes the cap.
