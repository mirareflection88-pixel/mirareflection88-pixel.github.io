One family of blockchains, running live.

The Twin Blockchain is a family of four coin chains — TWOPI, KEEL, AGORA and TESSERA — tied together by one hub. They produce blocks around the clock, and this page shows them as they are: how tall each chain stands, when its newest block landed, and how the family is joined together. No account, no sign-up — looking is free, and the door is open.

How fresh is this data?

Every number on this page comes from the chains' own published records, refreshed about every five minutes. The line at the top of the page always says when the data was updated. If an update is ever late, that line turns into a plain warning and the numbers are shown as the last ones published — never dressed up as live.

What the coloured dots mean

Green — the chain is producing and its published record was updated recently. Amber — the chain's last published record is older than usual; the numbers shown are the last ones published, with their times. Red — the chain is not publishing right now. The dot never guesses: it is read from the chain's own publication time.

Joined as one combination

The four chains are not four separate projects. Each coin chain is anchored to the family's hub: a fingerprint of the coin chain is written into the hub, where anyone can check it later. The combination was verified end to end on October 25, 2026, and the anchors of record are:

  • KEEL block 2,212 is anchored on the hub at block 12,224 — anchor transaction 7e08562dcb966671f89a601f86c946346895fd7e42d91f2997366a90fc982ebc
  • AGORA block 2,198 is anchored on the hub at block 12,222 — anchor transaction f9e5cb1755538a02b8775c560935bf9ad0f4f39b39a0109c3ea9749ad39072f7
  • TESSERA block 1,980 is anchored on the hub at block 12,225 — anchor transaction 804120b060bc1714d0e776b3da12d9d47d8f03792c201ab741d83101
  • TWOPI is the chain the combination was submitted from: its validator signed and sent the three anchors above to the hub on October 25, 2026.

Anchoring is how the family proves its chains belong together and have not been quietly rewritten.
