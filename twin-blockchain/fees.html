Fees — what Twin charges, and what it does not

Twin has two kinds of fees, and they are kept strictly separate. The core-chain fee is paid in a chain's own coin, on the chain, and is in force today. The service-layer fees are charged in US dollars for the services around the chains — creating and publishing a personal blockchain, hosting, anchoring — and every figure for them on this page is a proposal, labelled as one. A Twin fee that is not a published number or formula does not exist.

On this page: In force today · The proposed service schedule · What is fee-free · What fees never are

This page was last reviewed on November 3, 2026, against the family's fee record of that date.

In force today: the core-chain fee

Every transaction on the family's five chains carries a fee of 1 base unit — the smallest unit of the chain's own coin (1 coin = 1,000,000 base units).

100% of it is burned — destroyed, paid to no one. Not to Twin, not to the validators. The family's whole history so far stands at 6 transactions: 6 base units paid, 6 burned, 0 paid to any person.

Looking costs nothing: this website, the explorer and the chains' public records are free to read, with no account and no sign-up.

The proposed service schedule

Every figure below is PROPOSED. These amounts are the house's current proposals and have not been ruled. No service below is on sale, the joining door is not open, and no figure here is a price anyone can pay today. When a figure is ruled, this page will show it as in force, with its date. Service fees, when in force, are charged in US dollars or a US-dollar stablecoin.

What is fee-free — the whole list

Exactly two things, by ruling, and the list is exhaustive:

  • Donations.
  • The Citizens Wealth Organization — a separate organization, currently parked and not active.

Everything else the platform provides carries fees. That is deliberate: the fees are what power the platform itself, the same way fees work on Ethereum and Bitcoin.

What fees never are

  • No fee is a share of coin trading. Twin is not an exchange, runs no marketplace, and takes no percentage of coin sales.
  • No core-chain fee is paid to any person today — it is burned, in full.
  • No fee buys a verdict. The attestation gate applies the same published criteria to everyone, and the verified badge cannot be purchased.
  • No fee here is a price for a coin. The coins are not for sale, they carry no market price, and nothing on this page is an offer.
